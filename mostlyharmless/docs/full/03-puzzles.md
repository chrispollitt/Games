# Full game — puzzle chart with hint ladders

Format per puzzle: **ID — name (points)**, *situation*, **Solution**, alternates the parser must accept, `E:` easy-mode
assist, `F:` faithful-mode extra, and the four-rung hint ladder `H1` nudge → `H2` clearer → `H3` nearly the answer →
`H4` the answer. No puzzle can be failed permanently.

Totals: Act I 60, II 50, III 100, IV 90, V 60, VI 40 = **400**.

---

## Act I — Lamuella (60)

**1.1 Wake and look around (4).** *Hut.* Examine the sketch (2); take the jar with the Babel fish (2).
H1 The shelf holds things worth looking at. H2 Look at both objects on the shelf. H3 Take the jar; the fish matters later. H4 `EXAMINE SKETCH`, `TAKE JAR`.

**1.2 Pick the berries (3).** *Orchard Slope.* The bushes are thorny and the bees are working. **Solution:** wear the dressing gown
(its sleeves are thick) and use the basket; waiting a few turns for the bees to drift also works. `E:` auto-wear gown.
H1 The bushes bite. H2 Something you own is thick enough to protect your arms. H3 Wear the gown and take berries into the basket. H4 `WEAR GOWN`, `TAKE BERRIES` (basket in hand).

**1.3 The smoker's price (6).** *Smokehouse / River Bank.* Dunna will not give meat for coin. Her cleaver is blunt; she'll
trade meat for a sharpened edge. **Solution:** find the whetstone under the flat rock at the river bank (lift the rock), sharpen
the cleaver (or your knife). `F:` stone is in the water; it has to be dried first.
H1 Dunna cares about her tools. H2 Something sharp would please her. H3 The river has stones that could sharpen blades. H4 Lift the rock on the River Bank, take the whetstone, `SHARPEN CLEAVER WITH STONE`, give meat trade.

**1.4 Hob's order (10).** *Sandwich Hut.* Ask Hob; learn he wants meat and berries, no pickles; make and give it.
Parser: `MAKE HOB THE USUAL`, `MAKE A SANDWICH WITH MEAT AND BERRIES`. `E:` Hob volunteers the order.
H1 Ask the customer. H2 Hob has views about pickle. H3 Meat and sourberries only. H4 Put meat and berries on the board, `MAKE SANDWICH`, `GIVE SANDWICH TO HOB`.

**1.5 Tem's order (5).** *Lodge.* The Reeve has a sore tooth: he wants **soft** food (honey and herb curd, no crust).
H1 Look at how Tem eats. H2 He keeps touching his jaw. H3 Soft bread and honey, no hard crust. H4 Make a honey-and-curd sandwich on soft bread, `GIVE` to Tem.

**1.6 The parcel (5).** *Green.* The parcel is tied with a stiff cord. **Solution:** cut it with the knife (sharp from 1.3, or any knife),
open it, take the cube and read the note. Reading the note reveals the Mk II is not to be switched on.
H1 Something is tied shut. H2 You carry something that cuts. H3 `CUT STRING`. H4 `CUT STRING WITH KNIFE`, `OPEN PARCEL`, `READ NOTE`.

**1.7 Trillian and Random (3).** *Meadow.* The scene runs on its own; talk to Trillian for three exchanges (ASK about Random, about
Earth, about Ford).
H1 Trillian has something to tell you. H2 Ask her about her daughter. H3 Ask about Earth too. H4 `ASK TRILLIAN ABOUT RANDOM`, `... ABOUT FORD`.

**1.8 Feed Random (10).** *Sandwich Hut.* Random is hungry and hates being asked. **Solution:** sourberries and pickled roots (no meat).
Learn by watching what she picks off the plate; she prefers it unbuttered. Trust +1.
H1 Random won't be questioned. H2 Watch what she takes from your counter. H3 She eats the sharp and sour things. H4 `MAKE A SANDWICH WITH BERRIES AND PICKLES`, `GIVE SANDWICH TO RANDOM`.

**1.9 Ford's arrival (3).** *Meadow/Hut.* Ford turns up, hair on end. Talk to him twice; he explains the Guide, mentions the Vogons.
H1 Ford will explain. H2 Ask about the Guide. H3 Ask about the Vogons. H4 `ASK FORD ABOUT GUIDE`, `ASK FORD ABOUT VOGONS`.

**1.10 The pilot's break (11).** *Meadow.* The skiff pilot won't fly during his break. **Solution:** a sandwich of pickled roots only (his stomach).
Then `ENTER SKIFF` (3). `E:` pilot's hint on request.
H1 He's on his break. H2 What does a man on his break like? H3 He says he has strong views. H4 Pickled roots only. `MAKE SANDWICH WITH PICKLES`, `GIVE SANDWICH TO PILOT`, `ENTER SKIFF`.

## Act II — Hub Station (50)

**2.1 The form (10).** *Customs Hall.* Clerk Gruba demands Form 27B/6, completed, signed and stamped. The pen is in Lost Property,
the stamp is on Gruba's own desk (he stamps only forms already stamped). **Solution:** get the pen, fill the form (name, purpose "personal"),
then have Ford distract Gruba (2.4) while you stamp it.
H1 You need a pen. H2 Lost Property keeps things. H3 The stamp can only be used when Gruba looks away. H4 Fill the form, wait for Ford's distraction, `STAMP FORM`.

**2.2 The coin (6).** *Concourse.* The vending machine rejects every coin but refuses nothing it does not recognise. Hob's coin of no
known denomination works. Buys a platform ticket. `E:` machine prompts. `F:` coin is in the pocket of the gown.
H1 The machine is picky. H2 What do you have that nobody recognises? H3 Hob paid you in something. H4 `PUT COIN IN MACHINE`.

**2.3 The towel (8).** *Lost Property.* The Attendant returns items only to a person who describes them exactly. Arthur's towel
has a stain shaped like a country; the Mk II knows which one (too helpfully). **Solution:** describe it correctly; see 2.6.
H1 He wants a description. H2 Remember what was on the towel. H3 It had a stain. H4 `DESCRIBE TOWEL` with "stain" and "country".

**2.4 Ford's distraction (6).** *Customs.* Ford talks to Gruba about queueing theory. You need to wait until he says "and another thing".
H1 Let Ford do the talking. H2 Wait. H3 Watch Gruba's eyes. H4 `WAIT` until he turns to the window, then `STAMP FORM`.

**2.5 Clearance (10).** *Departure Gate.* The gate requires a probability clearance. The Mk II offers to file one. If you let it, the
clearance is granted too quickly and Ford gets suspicious (unlocks the Mk II subplot). Alternative: bribe the clearance desk with a
sandwich (honey) and get it honestly. Both paths pass; honest path gives full points.
H1 The desk wants something. H2 The cube offers help; do you trust it? H3 The clerk likes sandwiches. H4 `GIVE SANDWICH TO CLERK` (honey).

**2.6 First suspicion (5).** The Mk II answers a question before you ask it. Examine the cube after hearing it. Ford writes it down.
H1 The cube said something odd. H2 Examine it. H3 Ask Ford. H4 `ASK FORD ABOUT CUBE`.

**2.7 Guide entries (5).** Read five different Guide entries on Hub Station objects (1 point each).
H1 Things here are listed in the Guide. H2 Try the cube. H3 `CONSULT GUIDE ABOUT <object>`. H4 Do it five times.

## Act III — Guide HQ (100)

**3.1 Visitor badge (8).** *Lobby.* The terminal asks "purpose of visit". Honest answers are refused; the badge issues on any answer that
sounds like an administrative reason. Alternative: Ford's old pass in his drawer (3.5).
H1 The terminal wants a reason. H2 Plain truth won't work. H3 Make it sound official. H4 `TYPE "ROUTINE AUDIT"`.

**3.2 The scanner (8).** *Security Desk.* It flags the fish jar. **Solution:** leave the jar at the cloakroom (take a claim tag); the fish
cannot be lost. `F:` the attendant loses the tag once unless it is pinned.
H1 The scanner objects. H2 Some things shouldn't go in. H3 There's a cloakroom. H4 `GIVE JAR TO ATTENDANT`, take tag.

**3.3 Lifts (10).** *Lift Lobby.* One lift is cheerful, one sullen. The cheerful lift stops at the Editorial Floor only if complimented; the
sullen lift goes anywhere if you apologise for the cheerful lift. `E:` lift lists the rule.
H1 They have moods. H2 One likes praise. H3 The other likes sympathy. H4 `COMPLIMENT CHEERFUL LIFT` then `PRESS 12`.

**3.4 Zendra (8).** *Editorial Floor.* Ford's old colleague. She knows the Mk II is "wrong". Ask her about three topics (the Mk II,
Infinidim, Ford); she gives you the combination clue.
H1 She knows Ford. H2 Ask about the new Guide. H3 Ask about the owners. H4 `ASK ZENDRA ABOUT MK II`, `... ABOUT INFINIDIM`, `... ABOUT FORD`.

**3.5 Ford's desk (7).** *Ford's Old Office.* The desk drawer needs a three-digit combination. Zendra's clue is the number of entries Ford
wrote about Earth (the answer is *two*, then *three*, then *seven* as he kept revising). The drawer holds his old pass.
H1 The drawer is locked. H2 Zendra said something about numbers. H3 Count Ford's Earth entries. H4 Dial 2-3-7.

**3.6 The Earth entry (12).** *Archive.* Find the revision history of the Earth entry (from "Harmless" to "Mostly harmless"), and the
Infinidim memorandum in the drawer behind it: "all Earths into one afternoon". `F:` a drawer needs a key kept in a different stack.
H1 The Archive has the history of the entry. H2 Look in the stacks for Earth. H3 The memorandum is behind the revision file. H4 `SEARCH STACKS FOR EARTH`, `OPEN DRAWER`, `READ MEMORANDUM`.

**3.7 Vogon poetry (15).** *Server Floor.* The guard Orlo will let you pass for a poem. **Solution:** compose a verse (pick lines from three
pools of images; the worse the better). Orlo is moved to tears and lets you through.
H1 Orlo wants poetry. H2 It must be bad. H3 Pick sentiments with big, ugly words. H4 `COMPOSE POEM` — choose any three lines from the offered pools.

**3.8 Under the towel (12).** *Boardroom.* To overhear the Vogon editors, hide where they won't look: put the towel over your head (they treat
a person who cannot see them as unseen). The vent is the alternative route. `E:` Ford suggests it.
H1 They'll see you. H2 Remember the old rule about not being seen. H3 Your towel. H4 `PUT TOWEL ON HEAD`, `WAIT` through the meeting.

**3.9 The paradox (15).** *Server Floor.* The Mk II core answers any question with what you want to hear. Ask it a question you do not
want the answer to, ("What is the worst thing you could tell me?"), and it must tell the truth: the coordinates of the other London.
H1 The core flatters. H2 Make it say what you don't want. H3 Ask a question whose honest answer you dread. H4 `ASK CORE ABOUT WORST THING`.

**3.10 Out by the roof (5).** *Roof.* Alarms. Ford's ship is on the pad; Random's old trail leads to the same coordinates.
H1 Alarms are going. H2 Up. H3 The roof has a ship. H4 `GO UP`, `ENTER SHIP`.

## Act IV — London (90)

**4.1 Past the cordon (8).** *Cordon.* Police block the park; press can go through. **Solution:** borrow a press credential from the van (Press Pen),
wear it.
H1 Press are allowed in. H2 The van near the pen has spares. H3 Take a credential. H4 `TAKE CREDENTIAL`, `WEAR CREDENTIAL`.

**4.2 The trail (10).** *High Road / Studio.* Random's route is marked by small mishaps (a dropped ticket, a stolen kebab, a cat in a
bag). Follow them in order.
H1 She left a trail. H2 Look for small disasters. H3 The newsagent, the Tube, then the Studio. H4 `GO TO STUDIO` after checking the three clues.

**4.3 Trust (10).** *Anywhere.* Raise Random's trust to 4 by: returning her cube (if you took it), listening (ASK her about herself),
a correct sandwich, an apology. Never read her diary. Points are awarded at trust 2 and trust 4 (5 each).
H1 She doesn't trust you. H2 Listen more than you speak. H3 Ask about her. H4 `ASK RANDOM ABOUT HERSELF`, `GIVE SANDWICH TO RANDOM`, `SAY SORRY`.

**4.4 Tricia (12).** *Green Room.* Prove who you are: the Islington party, the mathematician who left with a man with two heads. Tricia
recognises the account because she did not leave. Pick the right details.
H1 She doesn't know you. H2 You share a night she never had. H3 The party. H4 `ASK TRICIA ABOUT PARTY`, `TELL TRICIA ABOUT PARTY`.

**4.5 Agrajag's trail (10).** *Anywhere.* A man in a raincoat appears in each place, always just leaving. Don't kill anything small (a fly, a snail).
Awards at the end of Act IV if no small creature has died (5) and if you speak to him once (5).
H1 Someone keeps turning up. H2 Be kind to small things. H3 He will be at the pub later. H4 Do not `KILL` any creature; `ASK MAN ABOUT HIMSELF`.

**4.6 The broadcast (12).** *Studio Floor.* Walk onto the live set and read the autocue message (Ford's script: "Don't Panic" and where to meet),
using Tricia's goodwill. `E:` Tricia hands you the cue card.
H1 The cameras are live. H2 The autocue is the key. H3 Tricia can give you air time. H4 `ASK TRICIA ABOUT BROADCAST`, `READ AUTOCUE`.

**4.7 The flat (8).** *Flat.* Diary, a framed photo, an earring. Match the earring to Trillian's (Act I). Awards for the match.
H1 This place tells you things. H2 Examine the earring. H3 Compare with your memory of Trillian. H4 `EXAMINE EARRING`, `EXAMINE PHOTO`.

**4.8 The Underground (10).** *Tube Station.* Random runs; you have to catch her on the right platform. The map on the wall is misprinted; the right
line is the one whose name sounds like the pub's.
H1 She went down. H2 Look at the map. H3 The name of the line. H4 `EXAMINE MAP`, `TAKE NORTHERN LINE` (or the one named in the map).

**4.9 Ford keeps up (10).** Ford cooperates at three beats (cordon, studio, Tube); each pays 3 points when you ask him the right question.
H1 Ford has ideas. H2 Ask him. H3 About the plan. H4 `ASK FORD ABOUT PLAN` at each location.

## Act V — The Dancing Gnu and the lawn (60)

**5.1 The table (5).** *Bar.* Get everyone seated: Tricia, Random, Ford at the corner table.
H1 They need somewhere to sit. H2 The corner. H3 Tell each to sit. H4 `TELL RANDOM TO SIT`, ...

**5.2 The man in the raincoat (15).** *Bar.* Agrajag's matchbook (see mini): he leaves it behind after the accident; reading it triggers the Act V decision.
H1 Look for something small on the floor. H2 After the commotion. H3 The matchbook. H4 `TAKE MATCHBOOK`, `READ MATCHBOOK`.

**5.3 The pistol (10).** *Bar/Snug.* Decided: **the accident always happens** (it is the book's pivot). The puzzle is to be ready for it.
Read the instruction card in the Snug (it tells you which way the beam swings), then tell Tricia, Ford and Random to get down just
before it goes off. Prepared, nobody is hurt and the matchbook is easy to reach; unprepared, the bar is chaos for several more turns
and the matchbook has to be found in the wreckage (a tighter timer before the Grebulons act).
H1 She has a dangerous toy. H2 There's a manual. H3 The Snug has the card, and the warning is worth giving. H4 `READ CARD`, then `TELL EVERYONE TO GET DOWN` before the click.

**5.4 The Grebulons (10).** *Lawn.* Talk to the Grebulons: they want a meal and a chart. They forgot what the button does. Learn that the button
destroys everything at once.
H1 There's a ship on the lawn. H2 Ask them about the button. H3 Offer food. H4 `GIVE SANDWICH TO GREBULON`, `ASK GREBULON ABOUT BUTTON`.

**5.5 The fish (10).** *Anywhere in Act V.* The Babel fish must be in your inventory. If you left it in the cloakroom (3.2), the tag reclaims it at the Plaza
before you go; the game checks this.
H1 The fish matters. H2 Is it with you? H3 The tag. H4 `GIVE TAG TO ATTENDANT` earlier; keep the jar.

**5.6 The choice (10).** *Bar.* With Random's trust ≥ 4, cube compliance ≤ 3, the matchbook read and Random, Tricia and Ford present, `PUT FISH IN EAR` (radio ending).
Otherwise the fish hesitates and the Earth is demolished. At the last moment the cube offers to "fix this for you"; refusing it is part of the solution.
H1 This is the moment. H2 What did the fish offer? And has the cube been steering you? H3 Do the people who matter have to be here, and do they trust you? H4 `PUT FISH IN EAR` with Random, Tricia and Ford present, having kept compliance low.

## Cube shortcuts (compliance)

Accepting one of these gives the puzzle's full points and adds 1 to the cube's compliance (see `01-story-and-systems.md`). The radio ending is closed at 4 or more,
so the player may take at most three. Solving the puzzle by hand costs nothing. The cube's suggestions are always in-world, and `/HINT` never counts.

| Puzzle | Shortcut the cube offers |
|---|---|
| 2.3 | Describes the towel for you (it knows the stain) |
| 2.5 | Files the probability clearance itself |
| 3.1 | Issues a visitor badge from its own credentials |
| 3.3 | Tells you each lift's rule |
| 3.5 | Reads out the desk combination |
| 4.1 | Prints a press credential |
| 4.2 | Points out Random's trail |
| 4.4 | Recites details of the Islington party to Tricia |
| 4.6 | Writes the autocue message for you |

Cameos placed in the puzzle chain (all optional, all brief): Marvin as the Hub Station announcement voice (2.x) and the sulking service robot at HQ (III);
Zaphod in a margin note on Ford's proofs (3.5), a London tabloid headline (High Road) and a star on the Grebulons' chart (5.4).

## As built (M4-M5): where the implementation differs from the chart above

- **Act I:** Random steals Ford's **ship**, not the cube; Arthur keeps the cube (it is needed for shortcuts in Acts II-III). Hob's coin is a real object, handed over when he is fed.
- **2.1:** the pen on a chain is in the **Customs Hall** (not Lost Property), so the form can be completed before Customs lets you through. `FILL FORM` (+4), then the stamp (+6) once Ford's distraction opens the window.
- **2.3:** `ASK ATTENDANT ABOUT NORWAY` (or fjords/coast) returns the towel; Ford's towel topic and the stain (Slartibartfast's coastline) lead there. Shortcut: `CONSULT GUIDE ABOUT TOWEL`.
- **2.5:** the honey sandwich can come from the player's own cooking or, as a no-dead-end fallback, the **unclaimed sandwich** in Lost Property. Shortcut: `CONSULT GUIDE ABOUT CLEARANCE`.
- **2.6:** the cube switches itself on in the skiff cabin if it was off, and writes the clerk's words before Gruba says them; `ASK FORD ABOUT CUBE` then scores.
- **2.7:** five Guide entries (hub station, customs, vending machine, Lost Property, the kiosk), 1 point each. Marvin's announcements play on the Concourse.
- **3.1:** `TYPE ROUTINE AUDIT` (any administrative word) at the Lobby terminal; or Ford's old pass at the security desk. Shortcut: the cube issues a badge.
- **3.2:** security guard **Kess** at the desk; **Pim** runs the cloakroom. The scanner flags the fish (jar or loose) and the knife. At the roof the jar is returned if it was left behind, so the fish can never be lost.
- **3.3:** `COMPLIMENT CHEERFUL LIFT`, or `APOLOGISE TO SULLEN LIFT`. A Sirius Cybernetics service robot in the corner is a Marvin-adjacent cameo.
- **3.5:** the combination is **1-2-5**, the word counts of the three Earth-entry drafts (Harmless / Mostly harmless / Mostly harmless. Bring a towel.). `DIAL 1 2 5`. Zaphod's margin note is on the proofs on the wall.
- **3.6:** `SEARCH STACKS` (+4), `OPEN LEDGER`, `UNLOCK DRAWER WITH KEY`, `OPEN DRAWER` (+3), `READ MEMO` (+5).
- **3.7:** **Orlo** guards the stairs on the Editorial Floor (not at the servers). `COMPOSE POEM`, then `POEM n n n`; the lines have an ugliness score, and 7 or more passes (5 in Easy).
- **3.8:** `WEAR TOWEL`, enter the Boardroom, wait; without the towel the Vogons politely escort you out.
- **3.9:** `ASK CORE ABOUT THE WORST` (or truth, dread, and so on) yields the coordinates.
- **3.10:** `ENTER SHIP` on the roof ends Act III for now.
- **Shortcut bits:** towel 1, clearance 2, badge 4, lifts 8, desk 16 (five shortcuts built so far; the chart's other four come with Act IV).

## As built (M6): Acts IV and V

- **Rooms:** Act IV has 10 rooms (the two Tube platforms are separate rooms), so the full game now has 44.
- **Flow of Act IV:** land in the Press Pen; take the spare credential from the van (`OPEN VAN`), wear it, cross the cordon (+8). Trail clues are objects to examine: the paper cup (+3), the kebab wrapper (+3), the ticket stub (+4). The man in the raincoat appears in four places; `ASK MAN ABOUT HIMSELF` (+5); killing a small creature (fly, snail, moth) spoils the other +5.
- **Tricia (+12):** `TELL TRICIA ABOUT` any two of: the Islington party / the man with two heads / astrophysics. On success the cube calls Random by name aloud and she bolts. Shortcut: `CONSULT GUIDE ABOUT TRICIA` (she is convinced, +1 compliance).
- **Broadcast (+12):** `ASK TRICIA ABOUT BROADCAST` after she is convinced, then `EXAMINE AUTOCUE` on the studio floor. Shortcut: the cube writes it.
- **Chase (+10):** Platform 2, as the map and the ticket stub both say; waiting is the right thing. If you take 12 turns Random comes back on her own, minus one trust and the points.
- **Trust:** feeding (Act I) +1; `ASK RANDOM ABOUT HERSELF` +1; `ASK RANDOM ABOUT HER MOTHER` +1; `APOLOGISE TO RANDOM` +1; `GIVE DIARY TO RANDOM` unread +1. Opening the diary costs 1. Trust 2 pays 5, trust 4 pays 5; compliance 4 or more caps trust at 3.
- **Act V:** `SIT` seats everyone (+5). The pistol accident always happens at the fourth turn in the bar; `EXAMINE CARD` in the Snug and `TELL EVERYONE TO GET DOWN` beforehand (+10, and a longer fuse). The matchbook is +15. The barman gives a sandwich; the Grebulons on the lawn give +5 for food and +5 for the button, and a Zaphod cameo on their chart. Fish carried into the bar: +10.
- **Choice (+10):** `PUT FISH IN EAR` needs the matchbook read, trust 4 or more, compliance 3 or less, and Random, Tricia and Ford present. Until Act VI exists, the radio ending stops at "There is a pop".
- **Scores:** Act IV ends at 300; the book endings top out at 350 (not 360 as first drafted); the radio route reaches 360 here and 400 with Act VI.
- **Cube shortcut bits:** credential 32, trail 64, Tricia 128, autocue 256 (nine shortcuts now built).

## As built (M8): safety nets, assists and test tooling

**No-dead-end safety nets added**
- Dropping an ingredient away from the sandwich hut returns it to its home; a forgotten sandwich in another room is cleared when you make the next one.
- If the towel was never claimed, Ford hands it to you at the start of Act III.
- If the coin was lost, Ford gives you another (`ASK FORD ABOUT THE COIN` at Hub Station).
- If the fish was left behind, the roof (Act III) or Ford (start of Act V) returns the jar.
- Random returns on her own if the Tube chase takes too long; the sketch is returned by Random at Milliways.

**Difficulty assists now implemented**
- *Easy:* gown and credential put on automatically; Hob volunteers his order; the vending machine and the building directory print their rule; Tricia clears the broadcast without being asked; the poem threshold is lower; the Customs window stays open longer; idle nudges after 20 stalled turns.
- *Faithful:* one hint rung per puzzle; the Act V fuse after the shot is two turns shorter.
- *Not implemented (stated honestly):* turning off the library's implicit takes, and extra red herrings.

**Input handling:** `DIAL 1-2-5`, `DIAL 125` and `POEM two two three` work; "what does Hob want?" is put to Hob.

**Test tooling:** `tests/soak.sh <game> <act> [runs] [length]` (random commands from a random point in each act's walkthrough: 270 runs found no interpreter errors); `tests/release.sh` (release builds, size report, release replay against goldens); walkthroughs `parser2`, `act3_alt`, `easy_act1`, `faithful_finale`.

**Sizes:** release full game `full.z8` is 225,792 bytes (limit for Z8: 524,288); the mini game is 134,144 bytes (Z5 limit 262,144).

## As built (post-M8): compound commands and save names

- **Compound commands** (`engine/compound.h`): `TAKE PARCEL AND EXAMINE IT`, `DROP ALL, LOOK`, `E; S`, `TAKE BERRIES AND N`. The standard library's own "AND followed by a verb" test is broken
  by operator precedence in this library version, so the engine rewrites such connectives to THEN before parsing. Tests: `tests/full/compound.cmd`, `tests/mini/compound.cmd`.
- **`/SAVE name`, `/RESTORE name`:** accepted; because a Z-machine story cannot choose the file name, the game reminds you to give that name to the interpreter's own prompt. Test: `tests/mini/saves.cmd`.
