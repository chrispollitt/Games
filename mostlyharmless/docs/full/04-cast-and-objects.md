# Full game — cast and key objects

Each NPC: **role**, **location by act**, **follower?**, **topic list (minimum eight)**, **quirks**. Every NPC also has a
fallback line that stays in character, and responds to `ASK X ABOUT <topic>`, `TELL X ABOUT <topic>`, `/talk`-style loose
phrasings via the engine. Topic names below are the nouns the player is likely to type.

## Principals

| NPC | Where | Follows? | Topics (min 8) | Quirks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ford Prefect** | Acts I–V | yes (can wait) | guide, vogons, cube/mk ii, towel, plan, earth, random, fenchurch, hob | Often wrong about dates; offers a risky idea before every difficult puzzle; says "Don't panic" at the worst moment. |
| **Random** | Acts I–V | yes (trust-gated) | herself, mother, father, food, cube, ship, earth, fish, sketch | Trust 0–5. At trust ≥ 2 she volunteers info; at ≥ 4 she holds your hand in Act V. Will run if cornered. |
