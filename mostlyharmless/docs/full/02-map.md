# Full game — map

42 rooms. Exits are compass directions plus UP/DOWN/IN/OUT. `GO TO <place>` works inside a region (the engine's WalkTo).
Each region has a **hub** room that `GO TO` routes through. `(F)` = a follower (Ford/Random) usually here.
Hazards: none that kill; the only timed section is Act V.

## Act I — Lamuella (8 rooms)

```
                Orchard Slope
                     |
 Smokehouse --- Village Green --- Reeve's Lodge
                     |              
  Arthur's Hut --- (green) --- Sandwich Hut
                     |
                 Landing Meadow --- River Bank
```
(Hut is W of the Green, Sandwich Hut E, Meadow S in this sketch; final compass layout is fixed at implementation.)

| Room | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Arthur's Hut** | bed, shelf with the pencil sketch and the jar (Babel fish), dressing gown, basket, the "sandwich licence" on the wall |
| **Village Green** (hub) | the parcel (arrives at the end of the morning), well, Hob loitering at times |
| **Sandwich Hut** | counter, board, knife, cool pit (pickled roots), bread bin, shelf of herbs; order book |
| **Orchard Slope** | sourberry bushes (thorny), bees; Tem's goat tethered here |
| **Smokehouse** | Dunna the smoker, hanging meat, a blunt cleaver; smoke is thick (visibility gag) |
| **River Bank** | reeds, a whetstone under a flat rock, stepping stones; Ford's ship lands near here later |
| **Reeve's Lodge** | Tem, the "Hearing Stone" (village rumours), a map of the region; first place Trillian is seen |
| **Landing Meadow** (gateway) | the mail skiff and pilot; the white shuttle (Trillian); Ford's ship |

## Act II — Hub Station (6 rooms)

```
 Skiff Cabin --- Arrivals --- Customs Hall --- Concourse --- Departure Gate
                                                  |
                                           Lost Property
```
| Room | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Skiff Cabin** | the pilot's seat, the instrument panel (the Mk II talks to it); transit scene only |
| **Arrivals** | a self-aware kiosk, a queue sign, a plant that has been waiting 11 years |
| **Customs Hall** | Clerk Gruba behind glass, Form 27B/6, a rubber stamp, the "Items to Declare" bin |
