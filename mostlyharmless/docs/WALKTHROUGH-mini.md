Notes
- Hob wants meat and sourberries, no pickles; Random eats sourberries and pickled roots; the pilot wants pickled roots only. Keep the Babel fish from your hut with you from the start.
- The accident with the pistol in the pub always happens. Reading the matchbook is what makes the happy ending possible. `PUT FISH IN EAR` needs the fish with you, the matchbook read, and Random fed and present.
- For a **book ending**, skip the fish: read the matchbook (a calmer end), or don't (the grimmest one).
- Asking the Guide about someone's order is a shortcut, and each one counts against you; four or more and the fish will refuse.
