# MOSTLY HARMLESS: THE LONG WAY ROUND — complete walkthrough

**Spoilers throughout.** This is one route that scores the full **400 points** and reaches the happy (radio-play) ending without any shortcuts from the Guide. It is the exact command
list the test suite replays (`tests/full/complete.cmd`), from the first move to the last, in 197 turns. Many other phrasings work: the game accepts plain English, typos,
and compound commands such as `TAKE PARCEL AND EXAMINE IT`.

Tips: `/HINT` gives a nudge (and `/HINT BACK` re-reads it); `/DIFFICULTY EASY` or `FAITHFUL` changes how much help you get; the Guide Mk II will offer shortcuts, and each one you accept
**adds to its hold on you**. Take three at most and the happy ending stays open; four or more closes it. The route below accepts none.
